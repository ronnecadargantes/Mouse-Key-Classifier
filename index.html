03 • Significance to the Community

Why Mouse & Keyboard Recognition Matters for Our Community

Everyday computer peripherals—the mouse and keyboard—are the primary bridge between people and digital opportunity. Making peripheral recognition and brand awareness accessible in the browser supports classrooms, community labs, and learners across every skill level.

01 / STUDENTS & SCHOOLS

Hands-On AI Literacy

Helps students see how machine learning works on familiar hardware—a mouse and keyboard—turning abstract AI concepts into tangible classroom learning.

02 / COMPUTER LABS

Hardware & Brand Tracking

Assists school laboratories, libraries, and community tech hubs in cataloging, inspecting, and identifying mouse and keyboard brands across workstations.

03 / ACCESSIBILITY & ERGONOMICS

Inclusive Input Awareness

Encourages thoughtful selection of ergonomic mouse shapes and comfortable keyboards to support learners with diverse physical and accessibility needs.

04 • Visual Craft & Usability

Principles of Design Applied

Every interface element is intentionally structured around core principles of design to keep scanning, brand checking, and reading effortless.

1. Visual Hierarchy

Fraunces display titles, Plus Jakarta Sans body copy, and JetBrains Mono probability readouts establish clear priority from top-level purpose to live scan metrics.

2. Contrast & Color Theory

A calm warm-stone background pairs with high-contrast slate ink, emerald accents for mouse identification, and cobalt accents for keyboard and brand telemetry.

3. Balance & Alignment

A 12-column responsive layout aligns camera controls, confidence bars, and brand verification cards cleanly across mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.

4. Proximity & Grouping

Camera toggles sit directly beside the live preview, while brand selection, category controls, and the session log button stay grouped in one workflow card.

5. Repetition & Consistency

Uniform rounded corners, consistent border tones, and repeatable card structures create predictable interactions across every section of the page.

6. White Space (Negative Space)

Generous outer margins, card padding, and vertical spacing between sections prevent visual crowding and keep focus on your scanned mouse or keyboard.
